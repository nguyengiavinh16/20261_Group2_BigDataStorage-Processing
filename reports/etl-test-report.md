## 5. Verification limits

- Rerun assertions compared processed IDs and expected counts, but did not compare
  every processed field value. Before/after snapshots were not retained for all
  four outputs, and full-dataset contents were not compared across executions.
  The results establish the tested ID/count behavior rather than complete
  equality of all logical values. Parquet byte layout and physical row order
  were not compared.
- Results apply to the recorded validation policy,
  `profiling_reference_pending_team_contract_review`. Duplicate rows, zero
  amounts, outliers, and negative balances are retained under this policy.
- Ingestion supports one local UTF-8 CSV with one record per physical line.
  Multiline CSV, HDFS, and distributed execution were outside the verified scope.
- `step` was retained without generating `event_time`. Replay field exclusion
  was tested at the ETL output level; producer integration and scoring behavior
  were not tested.
- Outputs are overwritten one directory at a time. Recovery after interruption
  and concurrent-write behavior were not tested. Recorded runtimes do not
  constitute a performance benchmark or SLA measurement.
- Full and sample manifests have `git_commit: null`. At report preparation,
  the ETL code SHA-256 matched the manifest
  (`8d6a770f8f24c9e4454d4f416a7e5b39098622245b8b2003345138cea5630798`),
  as did the schema SHA-256
  (`9da7453c0612d3581d86969549aae82f6c88ae741eea80c2936566e08501f7d4`).
  The unit-test log does not capture execution-time code/test checksums, exit
  codes, the Docker image digest, or Docker/Compose versions.
- Dataset and Parquet outputs are excluded from Git. Links under
  `data/processed/` require the corresponding local artifacts. The older failed
  `fixture-check` is excluded from the passing evidence in this report.
